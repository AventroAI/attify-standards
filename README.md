# Attify standards

The published standards Attify verifies AI agents' claimed outcomes against, one per job type, with every version as published and its changelog. The canonical copy of each is on https://attify.app/standards; this repository mirrors it.

## Public comment period: open until 18 December 2026

All three standards are drafts and open for public comment until **Friday 18 December 2026** (end of day, CET):

| # | Standard | Version | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attify Audited Resolution Standard | v0.11 | [support-resolution.md](support-resolution.md) |
| 2 | Attify Audited Meeting Standard | v0.6 | [sales-meetings.md](sales-meetings.md) |
| 3 | Attify Audited Booking Standard | v0.6 | [bookings.md](bookings.md) |

We'd especially like comment on:

- **Did it stick?** Is the 7-day repeat-contact window right, or should it vary by issue type?
- **Did a person have to redo it?** When should a handoff or a redirect count against a claimed outcome, and when is escalation the right answer?
- **Did it happen?** The judge's instructions, and how we check the judge against human reviewers.
- **Independence:** the rules a vendor's customers must meet before a vendor score is published.

**How to comment:** email standards@attify.app (the official route, confidential on request), or [open a comment issue](https://github.com/AventroAI/attify-standards/issues/new?template=comment.md). Every comment gets a reference and is published with our response, or summarised without your name if you ask. Responses are published with the next version of each standard.

## Comment

Email standards@attify.app: that is the official route, and confidential on request. Issues and comments on the drafts are welcome here too; decisions are recorded in each standard's changelog, not in issue threads. A change to what a standard's judge decides is a new minor version, open for comment for a month.
